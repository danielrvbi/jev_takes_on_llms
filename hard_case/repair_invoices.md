## E. Later shop explanations and records requested

Sarnel's 24 September email confirms the vehicle has not been painted or had parts replaced. The front bumper cover was released for photographs; the carrier was photographed in place. The wheel alignment sheet reports left-front toe 0.12 degrees outside the shop's tolerance. It does not show pre-incident alignment and does not itself prove when the condition arose. A generic diagnostic printout says “forward assist calibration recommended”; no error code or completed calibration is documented. The shop says the calibration allowance is based on a common post-repair procedure, but has not supplied the vehicle-specific manufacturer instruction.

The transporter docket records pick-up at Bellweather Ring on 14 September at 18:47 after police clearance, delivery at 19:12, and a charge of €145 net / €174 gross. It says “steering not straight; customer requested no road drive.” This supports a tow but does not show whether the policy's roadside assistance service arranged or reimbursed it. Storage is priced at €30 net per day from 16 September; an earlier email offered to waive storage if all work was authorized. We do not know whether the offer is still open or whether the charge was incurred.

The provisional amount exceeds the standard €450 excess before any coverage or scope decision. If the insured event is accepted, an excess would ordinarily be deducted once from the covered current-impact settlement; no deduction has yet been made. Group B is identified as prior damage and should not be folded into the current claim without evidence of a new worsening or necessary, proportionate blend. The difference between the several cover-sheet totals and line sums remains unresolved.
